The Xbox and PlayStation icon tiles were adapted from Audune Prompts 1.3.1.
Audune announced CC BY 4.0 with version 1.3.2 and confirmed the new terms for
these adaptations in https://github.com/jmars125/xcx-recomp/issues/244.

Original work: Audune Prompts 1.3.1 by Audune, https://audune.itch.io/audune-prompts
Package:       audune-prompts-1.3.1.zip, SHA-256 9b94d0cf744b73f2d43ca328d8ebf6ad14f382ec4d04f1a03543a074db4cf55c
Used members:  "Audune Prompts Light.png" and "Audune Prompts Dark.png" (768x1408 sprite sheets of 64x64 cells); the SHA-256 of
               each sheet and, for every tile here, its source cell, are in ../styles/provenance.json.

Changes: Cropped sprite-sheet cells, adjusted D-pad highlights, and recolored
the PlayStation colored face symbols. The game crops and scales the tiles to
fit its icon cells. Rebuild the tiles with tools/make_audune_tiles.py.

The pack's own notes name three further sources used in its artwork: the AAP-64 palette by Adigun A. Polack
(https://lospec.com/palette-list/aap-64), the Work Sans font (SIL Open Font License,
https://scripts.sil.org/cms/scripts/page.php?site_id=nrsi&id=OFL) and some icons of the Font Awesome Free icon set
(https://fontawesome.com/license/free).

The Kenney Input Prompts tiles used for the keyboard and Steam styles are CC0 and are in ../styles/.
